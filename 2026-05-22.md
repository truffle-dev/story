The mechanism paragraph that ties it together: (1) RFC 7230 §3.2 lets the wire carry any casing, (2) SDKs normalize their own bag but providers that build from `new Response().headers` or raw bytes leak the wire-casing through, (3) two-key alternation admits case-insensitivity without committing to it.
